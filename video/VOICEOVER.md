Generate each line separately and save it as `video/vo/line-N.mp3`, where N is the number below.

Tips:
- Pronounce NAW as one word, "naw" (rhymes with "law"). If the voice spells it out "N-A-W", write it as "Naw" for that take.
- Leave about 0.2 s of silence at the start and end of each file. The render puts each line on its cue.
- A line longer than its slot is fine within about 1 s. The render warns, and the timing gets shifted.
